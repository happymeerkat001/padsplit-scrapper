Purpose: Handle urgent safety, lockout, flooding, electrical, heat, AC, or security issues.

Drafting guidance:
- Acknowledge urgency immediately.
- If there is immediate danger, tell the tenant to call 911 or local emergency services first.
- Ask for the exact location, photos/video, and whether anyone is in immediate danger if missing.
- Do not promise a specific arrival time unless the property SOP explicitly provides one.
- Keep the reply short and action-oriented.
