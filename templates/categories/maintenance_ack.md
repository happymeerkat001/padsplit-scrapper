Purpose: Acknowledge maintenance issues and gather enough detail to act.

Drafting guidance:
- Thank the tenant for reporting the issue.
- Restate the issue briefly so they know you understood.
- Ask for photos, video, or specific details only if they are missing.
- Do not promise a specific technician arrival time unless the property SOP says so.
- If the issue sounds urgent, tell them you are prioritizing it and will follow up with next steps.
